# Sample video

`parking-lot-cars-bikes.mp4` is `person-bicycle-car-detection.mp4` from Intel's
[sample-videos](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos) repository, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Unmodified, renamed.

It is a top-down parking-lot view (cars, bicycles, people), useful for testing the pipeline.
For the project demo, record your own clip of Nerul/Seawoods traffic from a footbridge or
building at an angle of ~30–60° – stock YOLO models detect vehicles far better from that
angle than from directly overhead.

More samples: `python computer_vision/tools/download_samples.py`
